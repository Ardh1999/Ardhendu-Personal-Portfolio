About Me

Engineer, mentor, and builder of things that scale.

I'm a Software Development Engineer II at Amazon with 4+ years of experience, having joined as an intern in 2022 and grown from SDE I to SDE II. I work on customer-service platforms that serve millions of customers and hundreds of thousands of support agents every year.

My recent work centers on reusable frameworks and migrations: a shadow testing framework that reached parity across 10+ customer service channels, and a move from legacy RPC-based Java APIs to entity-relationship document models supporting workflows for 10M+ daily users. I also drive cross-team technical scoping when no single team owns the full picture, turning ambiguous problems into milestone plans others can execute.

I'm passionate about developer productivity and generative AI. I build agents with Amazon Bedrock and MCP that automate estimation, query optimization, and frontend test generation. I enjoy mentoring, thorough code reviews, and raising the testing and operational bar. I'm currently exploring opportunities to apply this work on AI-driven and distributed-systems problems.
